## The problem Afreximbank knows better than anyone

- Intra-African trade is **~15%** of the continent's total — AfCFTA's goal is to change that, and Afreximbank is its primary financier.
- But the **operational data layer beneath trade is fragmented, delayed, and untrusted**:
  - Port performance, berth utilisation, corridor transit times, and dwell costs are opaque or self-reported.
  - Project-monitoring for infrastructure loans relies on periodic manual reporting.
  - Trade statistics lag **12+ months** — useless for real-time decisions or covenant monitoring.
- You cannot **de-risk, finance, or measure the impact of** what you cannot observe with confidence.

<!-- Frame the problem as Afreximbank's problem, not a generic one. -->

## Why now

- **AfCFTA is operational** — and moving from ratification to implementation, where *measurement* becomes the bottleneck.
- **AfCFTA Digital Trade Protocol (2024)** demands interoperable, standards-based trade data.
- **Satellite AIS + earth observation** are now affordable enough to observe ports and vessels continuously — a capability that didn't exist at this cost five years ago.
- **DFIs are under pressure to prove development impact** (SDGs, Agenda 2063) with evidence, not narrative.

## What Sinapse XD is

A **federated trade intelligence platform** that fuses six external data pillars into a single, decision-ready layer — then goes one step further than a dashboard: it turns intelligence into **decisions, workflows and monitored outcomes**.

- **Six live data pillars:** AIS & vessels · Trade analytics · Market/freight · Weather & marine · Financial · SDG/development.
- **An ontology** where every real-world object — Country, Port, Corridor, Berth, Project, Commodity — is first-class and interconnected.
- **An evidence engine** where every KPI traces Bronze → Silver → Gold, with confidence and lineage.
- **A Decision OS** — recommendations, simulation, workflow and monitoring, not just charts.

## Appendix — Architecture

- **Lakehouse:** Bronze (raw, immutable, checksummed) → Silver (cleaned, ontology-keyed) → Gold (decision marts).
- **Ontology-first:** every object canonical and cross-referenced; queries traverse relationships, not tables.
- **Evidence on every value:** source system, transformation, validation, quality score, confidence, steward, timestamp.
- **Stack:** Next.js · Supabase (Postgres + Storage) · Upstash · Python ingestion workers · Anthropic Claude AI layer · Render hosting.
- **African data residency** by design; federated so raw port data never leaves the port's control.
